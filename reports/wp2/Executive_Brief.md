WP2 — Internationalisation of Kazakhstan Higher Education

## Purpose

To analyse Kazakhstan's international higher education ecosystem through student and faculty academic mobility (inbound and outbound), international agreements and grant programmes, migration context, and institutional internationalisation activity.

## Primary Research Question

How international is Kazakhstan's higher education system, and what opportunities does this create for future international engagement?

## Deliverables

- Analytical Report
- Executive Brief
- Interactive Dashboard
- Processed datasets (raw + cleaned)
- Figures
- Tables

## Status

In Progress — data collection and processing phase. See `09_TODO.md` for current task status and `07_Research_Assets_Register.md` for what already exists.

## Relationship to WP1

WP2 builds on the institutional and programme-level foundation established in WP1 (Higher Education Landscape). Where WP1 characterised *what* Kazakhstan's universities offer, WP2 characterises *how connected* that system is internationally — who studies where, which countries partner with Kazakhstan, and how that connectivity has changed over time.
