# Glossary

Terms used throughout WP2 (and carried forward for WP3 and the final integrated report).

**Academic Mobility**
International movement of students or academic staff for educational purposes, typically for a defined period rather than a full degree (contrast with "International Student" below).

**International Student**
A student enrolled outside their country of citizenship for a full degree programme (as opposed to a short-term mobility/exchange visit).

**Inbound / Incoming Mobility**
Academic mobility of foreign students or staff coming into Kazakhstan.

**Outbound / Outgoing Mobility**
Academic mobility of Kazakhstani students or staff going abroad.

**Migrant Stock**
Total foreign-born population residing in a country at a given point in time (a general demographic measure, not specific to students).

**Nostrification / Recognition**
The procedure for establishing the equivalence of a foreign educational document to Kazakhstani state educational standards.

**ENIC/NARIC**
European Network of Information Centres / National Academic Recognition Information Centres — the international network responsible for coordinating recognition of qualifications; Kazakhstan's national centre is ENIC-Kazakhstan.

**Bolashak Programme**
Kazakhstan's flagship state scholarship programme funding citizens to study abroad, with a contractual work-placement obligation upon return.

**Double Degree Programme (VDP)**
A joint programme in which a student receives degrees from two partner institutions (Kazakhstani and foreign) upon completion.

**Joint Educational Programme (SOP)**
A programme developed and delivered jointly by a Kazakhstani and a foreign institution (terminology as used in the CIS source report; full official expansion to be confirmed).

**ICM (International Credit Mobility)**
An Erasmus+ action funding short-term student/staff mobility between EU and partner-country institutions.

**PPVO**
Acronym used in the National Report 2024 for a specific Erasmus+ project category involving Kazakhstani and EU/associated-country universities; full expansion not yet confirmed against an authoritative source.

**Jean Monnet Actions**
An Erasmus+ strand supporting the teaching and research of European Union studies.

**Erasmus Mundus Design Measure (EMDM)**
An Erasmus+ mechanism supporting the design of joint international Master's programmes.

**Central Asian Higher Education Space (CAHES)**
A regional initiative (established 2021, formalised via the 2021 Turkistan Declaration) promoting cooperation among Central Asian higher education systems.

**Intergovernmental / Interdepartmental Grants**
Two distinct Kazakhstani government mechanisms funding citizens to study abroad under bilateral/multilateral international agreements, separate from the Bolashak programme.

**Bologna Process**
The intergovernmental reform process establishing the European Higher Education Area (EHEA), which Kazakhstan joined in 2010.

**Lisbon Recognition Convention (LRC)**
The 1997 Council of Europe convention on the recognition of higher education qualifications, ratified by Kazakhstan the same year.

**Tokyo Convention**
UNESCO's regional convention on recognition of qualifications in the Asia-Pacific region, ratified by Kazakhstan in January 2026.
