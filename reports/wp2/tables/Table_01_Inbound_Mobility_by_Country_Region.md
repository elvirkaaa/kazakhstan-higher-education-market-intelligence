# Table 1 — Inbound Mobility by Country and Region, 2021-2024

## Purpose
Provide the full country/region-level detail behind Figure 1 and Figure 3 (inbound side).

## Variables
- Country/region
- Year (2021-2024)
- Students

## Key Observation
CIS remains the dominant source region every year (788 of 1,207 in 2024), but Kyrgyzstan overtook Russia as the single top source country in 2024 (411 vs. 219) — a reversal from earlier years.

## Source
National Report on Higher Education 2024, Tables 6.1.3, 6.1.4, 6.1.5, 6.1.6.
