# Table 4 — Intergovernmental/Interdepartmental Grants by Country, 2024

## Purpose
Show which countries dominate Kazakhstan's two state-sponsored outbound grant channels.

## Variables
- Channel
- Country
- Candidates/grants

## Key Observation
Hungary and China lead both grant channels — neither channel's top destination is a CIS country, reinforcing the bifurcation pattern (Insight #6).

## Source
National Report on Higher Education 2024, Section 6.4.
