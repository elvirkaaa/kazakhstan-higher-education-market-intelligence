# Table 2 — Outgoing Mobility by Country and Region, 2021-2024

## Purpose
Provide the full country/region-level detail behind Figure 2 and Figure 3 (outbound side).

## Variables
- Region
- Year (2021-2024)
- Students

## Key Observation
Europe overtook CIS as the top outgoing destination region in 2023 and extended its lead in 2024 (2,360 vs. 1,402) — Turkey (792) and Poland (691) are the leading single countries.

## Source
