# Table 3 — English-Medium Provision by Subject Area

## Purpose

Identify which subject areas provide the deepest and shallowest English-medium catalogues nationally.

---

## Variables

- Subject area
- Number of programmes
- Number of distinct providers
- Number of English-medium programmes
- English-medium share

---

## Key Observation

English-medium provision is concentrated in a limited number of subject areas (led by Engineering & Technology and ICT); several fields remain predominantly non-English.

---

## Source

OPVO/EPVO Registry, consolidated dataset (2026).
