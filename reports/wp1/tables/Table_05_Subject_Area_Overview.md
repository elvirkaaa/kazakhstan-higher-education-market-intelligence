# Table 5 — Subject Area Overview and Competitive Intensity

## Purpose

Rank subject areas by number of distinct providers, to distinguish saturated fields from thin, low-competition fields.

---

## Variables

- Subject area
- Number of providers
- Number of programmes
- Mean tuition (KZT)
- Maximum tuition (KZT)
- Programmes per provider

---

## Key Observation

Business, Economics & Law and Engineering & Technology are the most contested fields by provider count; Architecture & Urban Planning and Healthcare & Medicine are the least contested.

---

## Source

OPVO/EPVO Registry, consolidated dataset (2026).
