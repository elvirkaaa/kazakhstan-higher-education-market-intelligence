# Glossary — WP3

**Natural Population Growth (Natural Increase)**
The difference between the number of births and the number of deaths in a population over a period, excluding migration.

**Total Dependency Ratio**
The ratio of the dependent population (aged 0-14 and 65+) to the working-age population (aged 15-64), per 100 working-age persons. A rising ratio means more dependents per working-age person.

**Total Fertility Rate (TFR)**
The average number of children a woman would have over her lifetime, given current age-specific fertility rates. A TFR of 2.1 is generally considered the "replacement level" in low-mortality countries.

**External Migration**
Migration across Kazakhstan's international borders (both arrivals into and departures from the country).

**Interregional Migration**
Migration between Kazakhstan's own regions (oblasts and republican-significance cities) — i.e. internal migration, distinct from external (international) migration.

**Net Migration / Migration Balance**
Arrivals minus departures over a period. A positive balance means more people entered than left; a negative balance means net outflow.

**Oblast**
A first-level administrative region of Kazakhstan (see the Data Dictionary "Administrative Regions" entry for the full list of 17 oblasts and 3 republican-significance cities).

**Youth (Kazakhstan official definition)**
Ages 14-28, per Kazakhstani national legislation and BNS statistical classification — distinct from the UN/Asian Development Bank definition (15-24) used elsewhere in this project. Both definitions are used in WP3 depending on which source is being cited.

**Working-Age Population**
In Kazakhstani statistical practice, defined with gender-specific thresholds: men aged 16 to 62(60), women aged 16 to 61(60) approximately (the exact BNS-published band is "16-62(60)"), reflecting Kazakhstan's gender-differentiated retirement ages.

**Age Pyramid (Population Pyramid)**
A graphical representation of a population's age and sex structure, typically shown as two horizontal bar charts (one per sex) stacked by age band.

**Medium-Fertility Variant**
The UN World Population Prospects' central/most-likely population projection scenario, based on an assumed medium path for future fertility rates. UN WPP also publishes high- and low-fertility variants, not used in this project.

**5-Year Age Band**
A method of grouping population data into 5-year intervals (e.g. 15-19, 20-24) rather than single years — the standard granularity published by UN WPP and, for most indicators, by BNS/TALDAU.

**Territorial Reform of 2022**
The creation of three new Kazakhstani regions (Abai, Zhetisu, Ulytau) on 8 June 2022 via splits from existing regions (East Kazakhstan, Almaty, and Karaganda respectively). This creates a structural break in all pre/post-2022 regional time series used in this project.
