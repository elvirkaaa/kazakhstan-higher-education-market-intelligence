# Table 3 — Population Projection, 2025-2050

## Purpose
Provide the full projection detail behind Figure 2, including secondary demographic indicators from the same projection.

## Variables
- Year, Population, Yearly % change, Net migrants, Median age, Fertility rate, Urban population %

## Key Observation
Kazakhstan's population is projected to grow by approximately 27.5% between 2025 and 2050 (20.8M to 26.5M), even as annual growth rate slows (1.09% in 2030 to 0.88% in 2050) and net migration remains persistently negative throughout the projection horizon.

## Source
Worldometer (www.worldometers.info), elaboration of United Nations, Department of Economic and Social Affairs, Population Division, World Population Prospects: The 2024 Revision (medium-fertility variant).
