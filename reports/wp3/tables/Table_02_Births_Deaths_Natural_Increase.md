# Table 2 — Births, Deaths, and Natural Population Increase, 1991-2025

## Purpose
Provide the full national natural-movement detail behind Figure 3 of the report.

## Variables
- Year
- Births
- Deaths
- Natural increase (births minus deaths, as reported by BNS)

## Key Observation
Births peaked around 2015-2020 (near or above 400,000/year) before declining toward 2025 (335,005) — natural increase in 2025 (200,270) remains well above the 1990s-2000s lows but below the 2015-2020 peak.

## Source
Bureau of National Statistics of the Republic of Kazakhstan, tables 612101 (Births), 612201 (Deaths), 612301 (Natural population growth).
