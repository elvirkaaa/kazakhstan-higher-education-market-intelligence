# Table 1 — Key National Demographic Indicators, 1990-2025

## Purpose
Provide a single reference table of Kazakhstan's headline demographic indicators across the full observed period, at 5-year intervals.

## Variables
- Year
- Total population
- Annual population growth (%)
- Urban population (%)
- Total fertility rate (births per woman)

## Key Observation
Population growth turned negative in the mid-1990s (-1.86% in 1995, the post-independence emigration trough) before recovering to a consistently positive rate from 2000 onward. Urbanisation and fertility both bottomed out around 2000-2005 before rising again through the 2010s-2020s.

## Source
World Bank Open Data, indicators SP.POP.TOTL, SP.POP.GROW, SP.URB.TOTL.IN.ZS, SP.DYN.TFRT.IN.
