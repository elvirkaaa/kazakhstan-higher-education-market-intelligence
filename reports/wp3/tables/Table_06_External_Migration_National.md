# Table 6 — External Migration Arrivals and Balance, 2000-2025

## Purpose
Provide the full national external-migration detail behind Figure 8.

## Variables
- Year, Arrivals, Net balance

## Key Observation
The external migration balance was sharply negative in 2000 (-108,307) — the tail end of the 1990s emigration wave — before turning positive in the mid-2000s and mid-2020s, though it remains volatile year to year rather than following a single clear trend.

## Source
Bureau of National Statistics of the Republic of Kazakhstan, tables 613201 (Arrivals) and 613203 (Balance of external migration for all flows).
