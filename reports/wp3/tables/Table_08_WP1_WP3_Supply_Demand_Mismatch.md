# Table 8 — Higher-Education Supply vs. Youth Population Share, by Region (WP1 × WP3)

## Purpose
Provide the exact figures underlying Figure 10 and the report's central finding (Section 9, Insights #12-16) in tabular form, for direct citation.

## Variables
- Region, Share of national HE programmes (WP1), Share of population aged 0-15 (WP3)

## Key Observation
Every region with an above-national-average youth share (Turkistan, Mangystau, Shymkent city, Kyzylorda, Atyrau) holds under 1.5% of national higher-education programme supply individually. Conversely, the two regions with the largest supply shares (Almaty, East Kazakhstan) both have below-average youth shares.
