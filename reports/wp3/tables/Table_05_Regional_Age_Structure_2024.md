# Table 5 — Regional Age Structure (3 Broad Bands), Beginning of 2024

## Purpose
Provide the full regional youth-share ranking underpinning the report's central finding (Section 9, Insights #12-16).

## Variables
- Region, Total population, Share aged 0-15 (%)

## Key Observation
Turkistan (39.5%), Mangystau (38.7%), Shymkent city (37.4%), Kyzylorda (36.4%), and Atyrau (35.8%) have the highest child-population shares nationally — all well above the 31.2% national average. North Kazakhstan (21.5%), Kostanay (22.6%), and East Kazakhstan (22.8%) have the lowest.

## Source
Bureau of National Statistics of the Republic of Kazakhstan, bulletin "Number of population of the Republic of Kazakhstan by gender and individual age groups at the beginning of 2024" (source code, transliterated: B-18-07-G).
