# Kazakhstan Higher Education Market Intelligence

> A comprehensive analytical research project exploring Kazakhstan's higher education sector, internationalisation, student mobility, demographic trends and market opportunities for international stakeholders.

---

## Project Overview

This repository contains an evidence-based analytical study of Kazakhstan's higher education system.

The project aims to combine institutional, demographic, economic and international education data into a single research framework that supports strategic decision-making for universities, educational organisations, researchers and international partners interested in Kazakhstan's higher education market.

Rather than presenting isolated datasets, the project integrates multiple sources to provide a comprehensive understanding of the higher education landscape.

---

Interactive Dashboard

View the live dashboard →

The primary output of this project is an interactive, browser-based market intelligence dashboard covering the completed work packages (WP1–WP3) and the cross-analysis that connects them:

Overview — the central research question and headline findings
Education Supply — regional and institutional concentration of Kazakhstan's higher-education programme registry (2,417 distinct programmes, deduplicated and verified from 8,530 raw records; regional HHI; subject-area and tuition breakdowns)
Internationalisation — inbound, outbound, and Bolashak scholarship mobility, kept as separate, non-equivalent channels rather than one combined figure
Demographic Demand — national and regional population trends, natural growth, and youth concentration
Supply × Demand — the project's central cross-analysis: correlation testing and a Regional Alignment Index comparing where education supply is concentrated against where demographic demand is growing
Key Findings — six findings, each broken into Finding → Evidence → Interpretation → Limitation
Appendix — full data pipeline, methodology, data-quality notes, and sources

The dashboard is built with vanilla HTML/CSS/JavaScript, D3.js, and Chart.js — no build step required. Open dashboards/index.html directly in a browser, or see Getting Started below for local setup.

Note: WP4 (Labour Market) and WP5 (Strategic Assessment) are not yet reflected in the dashboard — they require additional data collection before they can be included (see Repository Status).


## Research Objectives

The project focuses on answering several key questions:

- How is Kazakhstan's higher education system structured?
- What factors influence demand for higher education?
- How internationalised are Kazakhstan's universities?
- What are the major student mobility trends?
- Which demographic and economic factors affect higher education?
- What opportunities and challenges exist for international cooperation?

## Research Components

The project consists of five major analytical work packages.

### WP1 — Higher Education Landscape

Analysis of Kazakhstan's higher education system:

- universities
- educational programmes
- English-medium programmes
- institutional types
- regional distribution

---

### WP2 — Internationalisation

Analysis of international higher education:

- UNESCO data
- student mobility
- inbound students
- outbound students
- international partnerships

---

### WP3 — Demographic Context

Analysis of demographic indicators affecting higher education:

- population
- age structure
- urbanisation
- migration
- fertility
- regional demographics

---

### WP4 — Labour Market

Analysis of graduate outcomes and labour market:

- graduate employment
- salaries
- workforce demand
- economic indicators

---

### WP5 — Strategic Assessment

Evidence-based assessment of Kazakhstan's higher education market including:

- opportunities
- challenges
- regional strengths
- international competitiveness
- future outlook
