# WP1 — Higher Education Programmes Database

## Objective

To develop a comprehensive database of higher education programmes offered by universities in Kazakhstan, providing an overview of the national higher education landscape.

---

## Description

This research asset integrates programme-level information collected from the official EPVO Register.

The database supports institutional analysis, regional comparisons and identification of educational opportunities across Kazakhstan.

---

## Source

Official EPVO Register

---

## Current Status

Completed
