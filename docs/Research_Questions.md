# Research Questions

## Higher Education Landscape

- How is Kazakhstan's higher education system structured?
- How many higher education institutions operate in Kazakhstan?
- What institutional types exist?
- How are universities distributed across regions?
- Which institutions offer English-medium programmes?
- Which academic fields are most represented?

## Internationalisation

- How many international students study in Kazakhstan?
- Where do they come from?
- Where do Kazakh students study abroad?
- Which countries are Kazakhstan's major education partners?
- How has mobility changed over time?
- What demographic and migration trends may influence future student mobility?
- How competitive is Kazakhstan as an international education destination?

---

## Demographic Context

- How has Kazakhstan's population changed since 1990?
- What are future population projections?
- How is the youth population changing?
- Which regions are growing and which are shrinking?
- How does migration influence higher education demand?
- What implications does this have for universities?

## Labour Market

- How affordable is higher education?
- What salary outcomes exist?

## Strategic Questions
