Data coverage note: the free open boundary file used here (GADM-based, via open-data-kazakhstan/geo-boundaries-kz) reflects Kazakhstan's administrative structure before the 2018 Shymkent city status change and the 2022 creation of Abay, Zhetisu, and Ulytau regions. Astana, Shymkent, Abay, Zhetisu, Ulytau, and Turkistan (as a separate unit from the old "South Kazakhstan" oblast) are therefore shown in gray, not because their data is missing, but because this specific free boundary source cannot represent them as separate shapes. Their exact figures are still listed below the map.

Source: Bureau of National Statistics of the Republic of Kazakhstan, "Interregional population migration" table (data/processed/wp3/bns/KZ_Interregional_Migration_by_Region_Latest.xlsx in this project) — reference year not explicitly stated in the source, most likely 2024/2025. Region boundaries: open-data-kazakhstan/geo-boundaries-kz (GADM-derived, CC/public domain), loaded live from cdn.jsdelivr.net.
