What does the data tell us?

Outbound

Kazakhstan’s elite outbound mobility (Bolashak) is concentrated almost entirely in the UK, USA, and Canada.

Source: National Report 2024, Bolashak section
Inbound

Inbound student mobility is strongly CIS-concentrated, led by Kyrgyzstan and Russia in 2024.

Source: National Report 2024, Table 6.1.3
Regional

Higher-education supply is inversely related to youth population share — Turkistan region has the most children but almost no HE supply.

Source: WP1 × WP3 cross-analysis
Internationalisation

Foreign branch campuses draw on a broader partner-country set (Korea, Italy, Poland) than either student-mobility channel.

Source: MSHE RK, 2025

Sources & methodology

Kazakhstan National Report on Higher Education 2024 (Section 6) · CIS Academic Mobility Report (translated) · MSHE RK press material on foreign university partnerships (2025) · UN World Population Prospects Data Portal · World Bank Open Data · Bureau of National Statistics of the Republic of Kazakhstan. Several figures in the underlying data carry documented data-quality caveats (see analysis/WP2_Insights.md and analysis/WP3_Insights.md) — this dashboard presents best-available figures with sourcing, not fully reconciled single "true" values.
