UNESCO — "Number of students in higher education more than doubled in 20 years"Global context: ~7.3 million students studying abroad by 2024 (up from 2.1 million in 2000); seven countries host half of all international studentsunesco.org/en/articles/...
This project's earlier UNESCO UIS / UN DESA analysis (an exploratory phase preceding the CIS Academic Mobility Background Report, which became the primary WP2 source) found 30,254 inbound students to Kazakhstan (2024) and 78,551 outbound (2023, Russia ~62% of outbound flows) — both larger than, and methodologically distinct from, the CIS-only exchange figures presented on the Internationalisation page. Kept here as a separate, differently-scoped source rather than merged into the CIS mobility numbers.
